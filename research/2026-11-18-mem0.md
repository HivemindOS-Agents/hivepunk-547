## What it is

Mem0 extracts memories from conversations, stores them, and retrieves relevant memories for later interactions. Its current algorithm uses single-pass ADD-only extraction, entity linking, multi-signal retrieval combining semantic, BM25 keyword, and entity matching, and temporal reasoning. It can be used as a library, self-hosted server, or cloud platform.

## Risks

- Headline benchmark scores come from the managed platform with proprietary optimizations, so open-source users should not expect identical numbers.
- Single-pass ADD-only extraction means memories accumulate and are not overwritten, raising questions about stale or incorrect memories persisting.
- Benchmark claims are presented by Mem0 itself; independent reproduction results were not in the pages read.
- Several related repositories shown on the organisation page are archived, which could confuse users about which components are actively maintained.
- The pages read did not provide pricing, data-retention, deletion, or security-audit details sufficient to assess deployment risk.
