open-index/hacker-news is a Hugging Face dataset holding a full archive of Hacker News items (stories, comments, polls, job posts) from October 2006 onward, organised into monthly Parquet files with recent activity in 5-minute blocks. It is worth knowing because a third-party page describes it as a live mirror of Hacker News with real-time updates, which makes it useful for anyone who needs HN data they cannot get from the site directly.
